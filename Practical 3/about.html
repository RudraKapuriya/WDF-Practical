About Vidya Setu

A student-friendly academic information portal.

What is Vidya Setu?

Vidya Setu Student Hub is a simple web portal concept created to keep common student information together. Students can use it to check attendance, results, fees and college announcements.

The name Vidya Setu means a bridge to education and represents the connection between students and their academic information.

What students can do

  • Check attendance subject by subject.
  • View semester marks and grades.
  • Check fee payment information.
  • Read recent college announcements.
  • Contact the support office.

Simple by design

The portal is designed with clear navigation and simple sections so that students can find information without too many steps.
