Our Mission
Vidya Setu Student Hub is designed to streamline academic administrative operations, bridging communication gaps between students, faculty, and institute administrators through digital integration.
Key Features
  • Real-time attendance updates and shortage threshold tracking
  • Instant grade card publishing with SPI/CPI calculation
  • Integrated fee payment gateway and electronic receipt archives
  • Centralized digital notice board for emergency circulars
